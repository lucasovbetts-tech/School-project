Costa Coffee is a popular coffee shop brand that was founded in London in 1971. It offers a wide range of hot and cold drinks, including coffees, teas and hot chocolates, as well as sandwiches, cakes and other snacks. The Costa app allows customers to browse the menu, order drinks, collect loyalty points and find their nearest store. It aims to make buying coffee quicker, easier and more convenient for customers.
